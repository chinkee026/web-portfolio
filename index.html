About Chinx

Hi! I'm Chinx. I'm not really a gamer, but I've been loyal to only one game for years: Ragnarok Mobile Eternal Love. It's my stress reliever and my go-to escape when I'm not teaching, researching, or doing small freelance projects for friends. I've been away from hardcore programming and software development for about ten years, but I like to think I'm still developing something important — students. As an IT and Computer Science instructor, I teach, guide, debug, and sometimes play the role of tech support and motivational speaker at the same time. I also enjoy doing research, data analysis, and statistics, because numbers have their own stories and I love figuring out what they mean. My path shifted from building systems to building people, and it has been both challenging and rewarding in the best way possible.
